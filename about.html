About Me

Still learning. Still Building. Moving On.

I’m a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology working student at St. Dominic College of Asia, trying to make the most of every opportunity to gain experience, build, and improve. Balancing work and school hasn’t always been easy, but it has taught me patience, discipline, and the importance of showing up even when things get difficult. I’m not someone who knows everything, and I don’t expect to. I believe growth comes from asking questions, making mistakes, figuring things out, and being willing to try again.

My journey hasn’t been limited to IT. During the COVID-19 years, I also pursued nursing and emergency assistance training, gaining exposure to healthcare services, nursing assistant practicum, basic nursing skills, IV cannulation and injection, and life-saving response. Those experiences gave me a different perspective on helping people and reminded me that knowledge becomes more meaningful when you can use it to make a difference.

What I’m Building

My portfolio is a collection of what I’ve learned, what I’m working on, and what I hope to become better at. It includes projects involving web development, databases, networking, mobile applications, anti-fraud systems, and an IoT capstone focused on restroom sanitation, data analytics, and predictive maintenance.

I prefer exploring how things work behind the scenes, from designing an interface that people can actually use to connecting different parts of an application and understanding how devices, servers, and data communicate. I’m particularly interested in the balance between making technology functional and making it useful to the people who depend on it.

Some projects come together smoothly; others require more troubleshooting, research, and patience than expected. I see both as part of the process. Every challenge gives me a chance to understand something better, and every finished project gives me something new to build on. Along the way, I continue to develop my skills through hands-on practice, technical certifications, independent learning, and research.

Where I’m Headed

I’m working toward a career in IT where I can understand more than just the application on the screen.I want to explore the infrastructure behind it: data centers, wireless infrastructure, servers and network management, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

I know there’s still a lot for me to learn, and that’s part of what keeps me going. Technology never stays still, so neither can the people who work with it. My goal is to keep improving, gain meaningful experience, and work with people who take part in solving real-world problems.

This portfolio isn’t a claim that I’ve mastered everything. It’s a record of where I am right now, the work I’ve put in, and the direction I’m moving to.

I’m still learning, still figuring things out, still building. Still making every journey worth it.
