About Me

I’m a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology working student at St. Dominic College of Asia, building my skills one project and challenge at a time. Balancing work and study has taught me persistence, adaptability, and how to keep moving forward when the schedule gets busy. During the COVID years, I also pursued nursing and emergency assistance training, from healthcare services and nursing assistant practicum to basic nursing skills, IV cannulation and injection, and life-saving response. That experience broadened my perspective: practical skills and a willingness to help matter in every field. Away from the screen, I recharge with a good book or an evening motorcycle ride, two different ways to explore new perspectives.


My portfolio reflects the range of work I’m growing toward: web development, databases, networking, mobile apps, and an IoT capstone project focused on restroom sanitation, analytics, and predictive maintenance. I enjoy turning ideas into practical, user-friendly experiences, whether that means shaping an interface, connecting application components, or exploring how devices and data can work together. Alongside my IT studies, my training in COVID-19 preventive measures, emergency assistance, and hands-on healthcare taught me to stay attentive, learn procedures carefully, and think about how solutions help people. I continue to build experience through technical certifications and hands-on learning.


Looking ahead, I’m especially interested in data centers, wireless architecture, server and network management, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence. I’m motivated by steady progress and I look forward to collaborating with people who enjoy solving real problems with technology. Technology is the future. It is constantly evolving, and learning is lifelong.
