About Me

Still learning. Still Building. Where I'm Headed.

I’m a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology working student at St. Dominic College of Asia, making the most of every opportunity to learn, create, and improve. Balancing work and studies has taught me patience, discipline, and the importance of moving forward even when things get difficult. I don't claim to know everything. I believe growth comes from curiosity, learning through mistakes, solving problems, and being willing to try again.

My educational journey has taken me through different fields. Before pursuing a degree in IT, I studied BS Accountancy, where I gained foundational knowledge in Financial Accounting and Reporting, Cost Accounting, Business Law, and Taxation. These subjects introduced me to financial principles, business operations, analytical thinking and a sales career. During the COVID-19 years, I also pursued healthcare training, gaining practical experience in healthcare services, nursing assistance, and life-saving response. Each experience gave me a different perspective and taught me something valuable about responsibility, problem-solving, and helping others.

What I’m Building

This portfolio brings together what I've learned, what I'm currently working on, and what I hope to improve. It features projects in web development, databases, networking, mobile applications, fraud detection, and an IoT capstone focused on restroom sanitation, data analytics, and predictive maintenance.

I enjoy exploring how things work behind the scenes, from designing user-friendly interfaces to connecting applications, databases, servers, and devices. I'm focusing on building technology that goes beyond looking good or simply working; it should also serve a practical purpose.

Not every project comes together easily. Some require more research, troubleshooting, and patience than expected. But that's part of learning. Every challenge opens a new perspective, and every completed project gives me something to build on.

Where I’m Headed

I'm working toward a career in IT that goes beyond application development. I'm interested in exploring the infrastructure that keeps technology running, including data centers, wireless infrastructure, server and network management, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

I know there's still much to learn, and that's what keeps me motivated. My goal is to gain meaningful experience, continue developing my technical skills, and contribute to solving real-world problems through practical and responsible use of technology and AI.

This portfolio isn't about having everything figured out. It's about the experiences that shaped me, the skills I'm developing, and the direction I'm continuing to pursue.
