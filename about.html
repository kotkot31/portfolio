About Me

Still learning. Still Building. Where I'm Headed.

I’m a BSIT working student at St. Dominic College of Asia, learning to build, create, and improve while balancing work and school. That balance has taught me patience, discipline, and how to keep moving when things get hard.

Before IT, I studied BS Accountancy, building a foundation in financial reporting, cost accounting, business law, and taxation. It sharpened my analytical thinking and led me into sales, where I learned to understand people as much as numbers. During the COVID-19 years, I also trained in healthcare, from nursing assistance to life-saving response, which taught me responsibility, teamwork and care.

What I’m Building

This portfolio brings together what I’ve learned, what I’m working on now, and where I hope to grow. It spans web development, databases, networking, mobile applications, business systems, and fraud detection, along with my IoT capstone on restroom sanitation, data analytics, and predictive maintenance.

I welcome exploring how things work behind the scenes, from designing interfaces that feel intuitive to connecting applications, databases, servers, and devices into one working system. I want to build technology that does more than look good or simply run. It should solve a real problem and serve a real purpose.

Not every project comes together easily. Some demand extensive research, troubleshooting, and patience than expected. But that’s part of learning. Every challenge gives me a new perspective, and every finished project gives me something to build on.

Where I’m Headed

I’m working toward a career in IT that goes beyond application development. I’m drawn to the infrastructure that keeps technology running: data centers, wireless networks, servers, network management, cybersecurity, and the integration of artificial intelligence.

I know there’s still much to learn, and that’s exactly what keeps me motivated. My goal is to gain meaningful experience, keep sharpening my technical skills, and help solve real-world problems through the practical and responsible use of technology.

This portfolio isn’t about having everything figured out. It’s about the experiences that shaped me, the skills I’m building, and the direction I’m moving toward.
