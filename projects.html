KLIR: An IoT-Based Toilet Sanitation, Analytics, and Predictive Maintenance Management System

Introduction

Restroom sanitation is an important part of facility management because restrooms are shared spaces that are used repeatedly throughout the day. In educational institutions, the condition of these facilities affects hygiene, user comfort, public health, and the overall perception of campus services. Traditional restroom management usually depends on manual flushing, fixed cleaning schedules, and visual inspection. These methods can work for basic operations, but they do not provide a clear picture of actual usage, water consumption, device condition, or the exact time when cleaning attention is needed.

Objectives

  • Automate the toilet lid and sensor-triggered flush cycle, followed by UV-C disinfection.
  • Monitor usage, flush activity, water consumption, and device status through a cloud dashboard.
  • Use maintenance counters and alerts to identify servicing needs early and reduce downtime.
