About Me

Still learning. Still building. Where to next?

I’m a BSIT working student at St. Dominic College of Asia, learning to create, build and improve while balancing work, school and family life. That balance has taught me discipline, resilience and how to keep on trying when times get hard.

Before IT, I studied BS Accountancy, building a foundation in financial reporting, cost accounting, business law, and taxation. It sharpened my analytical thinking and led me into sales, where I learned to understand people as much as finance. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I trained in healthcare, from nursing assistance to life-saving response, which taught me teamwork, responsibility and care.

What I’m Building

This portfolio brings together what I’ve learned, what I’m currently working on. It spans across web development, network management, mobile applications, rdbms, to fraud detection and analytics.

I welcome exploring how things work behind the scenes, from designing intuitive interfaces to connecting applications and devices into one working ecosystem. I plan to build technology that does more than look good or simply run. It should solve a real problem and serve a real purpose.

Projects doesn't come together easily. Most demand extensive research, troubleshooting, and patience than expected. Every challenge gives me a new perspective, and every finished project gives me something else to build on.

Where to next

I’m working toward a career in IT that goes beyond application development. I’m drawn to the infrastructure that keeps technology functioning effectively: data centers, wireless networks, cybersecurity, cloud computing and the integration of artificial intelligence.

I know there’s still so much to learn. My goal is to gain meaningful experience, keep expanding my technical skills, and help solve real-world challenges through practical and responsible use of technology.

This portfolio isn’t about having everything figured out. I believe no one can do that. It’s about experiences. And if by any chance you read all of this, thank you. I hope we could, together or not, build something that makes a difference.
